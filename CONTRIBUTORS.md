## Levels and permissions

| Level | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Participant | Open issues, submit test evidence, request a task claim, and open pull requests from a fork. |
| Recognized contributor | Participant permissions and inclusion in the report for verified contributions. This level starts with the first active points award. |
| Maintainer nominee | The project opens a nomination after reviewing the contributor's record and receiving their consent. Existing permissions apply until the nomination is approved. |
| Maintainer and core contributor | Review pull requests and vote within the recorded delegation. Committee membership management and changes to protected files require separate permissions. |
| Owner | Manage governance, delegations, and repository protection. The owner votes when registered as a committee member. |

**Contribution share** = `100 × contributor account balance ÷ sum of active balances across all accounts in the project`. Display the result to two decimal places and show “Not calculated” if the total is zero. The percentage measures a share of verified contribution points. [Points policy](.pub-develop/points.md)

## Reputation records

- **Professional experience** is a private, descriptive record reviewed by a maintainer or an agent authorized for that governance task. Use GitHub's public API and relevant public examples of work. A participant may submit a LinkedIn URL or selected work samples. Review LinkedIn material supplied by the participant or accessed through a method the platform authorizes. Record the role-relevant evidence, date, and limits of verification. An absent or private LinkedIn profile leaves that source unavailable. [GitHub public profile API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/users/users#get-a-user) · [LinkedIn User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement)
- **Participation reputation** is a separate balance earned through contributions to this project. The public contributor report lists the GitHub account, level, delegations, points, and contribution share. Keep LinkedIn URLs and professional notes in a private record with limited access. Participants can request corrections or removal.

The runner opens a record when a participant first enters the project's workflow, keyed by `(repository_id, github_user_id)`. It queues the experience review for maintainers with status `pending`. The review can reopen when new evidence is submitted or before a nomination. Participants can continue contributing while their review is pending.

Treat résumés, profiles, URLs, and external work as evidence to verify. Limit the review to skills relevant to the task and verifiable work, and keep personal data and sensitive attributes out of the public report. The [committee policy](.pub-develop/committee.md) defines nomination requirements and who can approve promotions.
